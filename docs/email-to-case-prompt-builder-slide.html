Email-to-Case Automation with Salesforce Prompt Builder

AI Summarization, Routing & Reply Drafting
About the Client

High-volume Salesforce Service Cloud support operation in a regulated industry. Email-to-Case is live in production, with Prompt Builder templates summarizing, classifying, and drafting on every inbound case.

Challenge

Every inbound email was read, interpreted, categorized, and answered by a person, so cost-to-serve scaled one-for-one with case volume.

  • Agents spent the opening minutes of each case reading threads and deciding where it belonged.
  • Misrouted cases were re-queued and handled twice, driving rework and SLA breaches.
  • Replies were written from scratch, so tone and quality varied by agent and tenure.
  • Adding volume meant adding headcount, the only lever available.
Solution

Built entirely on tooling the client already licenses: Email-to-Case, Prompt Builder, Flow, and Omni-Channel. The automation runs inside Salesforce with no new vendor or platform spend.

  1. Capture with no keying: the inbound email creates the Case, carries the thread and attachments, and matches Contact and Account automatically.
  2. Summarize on create: a record-triggered Flow calls a prompt template grounded in the email body, case fields, and related records; summary, intent, and urgency write back to the Case.
  3. Classify and route once: the same grounded output sets Type, Sub-type, and Priority, and Omni-Channel sends the case to the right queue on the first pass, and the manual triage step disappears.
  4. Draft the reply: a second template composes a response from case data, entitlements, and approved Knowledge articles. Agents review, adjust, and send. Nothing auto-sends.
  5. Grounded or nothing: when the template cannot ground an answer it writes no draft, flags the Case, and routes to a specialist queue. Einstein Trust Layer masks PII and PHI with zero data retention.
  6. Tuned from real edits: agent edits and rejections are captured and fed back into prompt and Knowledge refinement each sprint.
Results
12 → 4 min
Average handle time per email case, with triage and first-draft writing removed from the agent's path
~70%
Less time to first touch; cases reach the right queue without a human triage pass
90%+

~85%

The cost lever: handling time, not volume, sets the size of the support team. At three times the throughput per agent, the same desk absorbs growth without new hires, or the same volume is served by a materially smaller team.
